My research studies pricing, incentive, information, and governance design in digital marketplaces, online communities, and access-oriented markets. I use empirical analysis, stochastic modeling, and game theory to understand how platform rules shape participation, service quality, and welfare.

<section class="research-bucket" aria-labelledby="policy-decisions-heading">
  <h2 id="policy-decisions-heading">How should we make decisions when policies change behavior and information?</h2>
  <ul class="research-list">
    <li>
      <h3>Selective Labels and Counterfactuals: Why Diagnostics Are Not Enough</h3>
      <p class="research-question">What can we learn about counterfactual outcomes when past human decisions determine which outcomes are observed?</p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Learning with Selectively Observed Treatments</h3>
      <p class="research-question">What can we learn when the treatment itself is observed only when the outcome is observed?</p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>The Winner's Curse: Why Optimizing Structural Models Overpromises and Underdelivers</h3>
      <p class="research-question">How should we evaluate a policy generated by an optimized structural model?</p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3><a class="paper-title-link" href="{{ site.baseurl }}/research/rethinking-algorithmic-fairness/">Rethinking Algorithmic Fairness for Human-AI Collaboration</a></h3>
      <p class="research-question">How should algorithmic recommendations account for biased human compliance so the combined human&ndash;AI system remains fairer than human decisions alone?</p>
    </li>
  </ul>
</section>

<section class="research-bucket" aria-labelledby="platform-rules-heading">
  <h2 id="platform-rules-heading">How do platform rules and new technologies reshape participation and coordination?</h2>
  <ul class="research-list">
    <li>
      <h3>Dynamic Scheduling of Nursing Home Inspections Under Strategic Incentives</h3>
      <p class="research-question">How should a capacity-constrained regulator schedule inspections when inspections both reveal latent quality and change providers' future quality choices?</p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3><a class="paper-title-link" href="{{ site.baseurl }}/research/the-empty-promise/">The Empty Promise: When Do P2P Platforms Fail to Serve Advance-Booking Customers?</a></h3>
      <p class="research-question">Why can dynamic pricing destabilize advance-booking markets, and how can platforms prevent those markets from collapsing?</p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3><a class="paper-title-link" href="{{ site.baseurl }}/research/partial-information-cloud-kitchens/">Partial Information in Fork&ndash;Join Operations: Evidence from Multi-Brand Cloud Kitchens</a></h3>
      <p class="research-question">How should food-delivery platforms form cross-brand bundles when combining orders creates delay costs for different customers?</p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3><a class="paper-title-link" href="{{ site.baseurl }}/research/structuring-online-communities/">Structuring Online Communities</a></h3>
      <p class="research-question">How do participation costs shape knowledge production, consumption, and the long-run size of an online community?</p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3><a class="paper-title-link" href="{{ site.baseurl }}/research/what-remains-after-llms/">Generative AI Shifts Technical Knowledge Production Toward Recombinant Novelty</a></h3>
      <p class="research-question">Which kinds of human-generated knowledge persist after generative AI, and how does AI reshape the structure of knowledge production?</p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3><a class="paper-title-link" href="{{ site.baseurl }}/research/payment-for-results/">Payment for Results: Funding Non-Profit Operations</a></h3>

    <li>

      <p class="research-question">When should a lender wait for a low-income borrower to recover rather than repossess and redeploy a productive asset?</p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3><a class="paper-title-link" href="{{ site.baseurl }}/research/scaling-sharing-platforms/">Scaling Sharing Platforms with Supply Constraints with Lease-to-Earn Contracts</a></h3>
